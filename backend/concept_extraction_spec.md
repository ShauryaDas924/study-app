# Concept Extraction System v1

## Goal
Convert student notes into structured, testable concepts.

Concepts are used for:
- mastery tracking
- targeted question generation
- weakness detection
- study planning

---

## Definition of Concept

A concept is:
- a principle
- a rule
- a method
- a relationship

It must be testable and learnable.

---

## Pipeline

1) Student writes notes  
2) AI extracts concepts  
3) Filter low-confidence concepts  
4) Store in DB  
5) Link to notes/questions/attempts
